Short answer

Roth IRA for college students

A Roth IRA is a retirement account funded with after-tax money. If you have earned income and qualify, it can give long-term investments room to grow tax-free for retirement.

Key takeaways
  • Roth IRA is the account.
  • ETFs or funds are the investments inside.
  • Earned income and IRS limits matter.

Simple example

A student with part-time earned income may be able to contribute up to the allowed limit or earned income amount, whichever is lower.

Common mistakes

  • Opening the account but never investing.
  • Confusing contribution limits with goals.
  • Choosing random stocks before learning basics.
