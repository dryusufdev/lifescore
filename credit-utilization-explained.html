Short answer

Credit utilization explained

Credit utilization is how much of your credit limit is being used. Lower reported balances usually look cleaner than maxed-out cards.

Key takeaways
  • Under 30% is a common starter rule.
  • Under 10% can look cleaner when optimizing.
  • Never pay interest just to show usage.

Plain-English guide

Keep the system simple enough to repeat.

01

Know your credit limit.

02

Keep spending controlled before the statement closes.

03

Pay down after the statement posts and before the due date.

Simple example

On a $500 limit, a $50 reported balance is 10%. A $400 reported balance is 80% and looks much riskier.

Common mistakes

  • Letting a low limit look maxed out.
  • Chasing rewards with high balances.
  • Opening cards just to change utilization.

FAQ

It is not debt, but some people let a small balance report when optimizing. Keep it simple.

It can update when issuers report new balances.
