Short answer

College guy closet basics

A good starter closet is built from repeatable basics: clean tees, good shoes, simple pants, layers, socks, underwear, and one sharper outfit.

Key takeaways
  • Fit matters before logos.
  • Start with shoes and pants.
  • Buy repeatable basics before statement pieces.

Plain-English guide

Keep the system simple enough to repeat.

01

Keep neutral tees stocked.

02

Own daily sneakers, casual shoes, and dress shoes.

03

Build around clean silhouettes.

Simple example

White tee, straight pants, daily sneakers, clean glasses, and a simple watch can look better than loud brands worn badly.

Common mistakes

  • Buying trend pieces before basics.
  • Ignoring tailoring and pant break.
  • Keeping worn-out underwear and socks.

FAQ

Start with daily sneakers, casual shoes, and one dressier pair.

White, black, navy, gray, brown, denim, and one accent.
