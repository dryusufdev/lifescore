Short answer

How to build credit at 18

At 18, build credit by opening one beginner-friendly card or becoming an authorized user, spending only what you can afford, and paying on time every month.

Key takeaways
  • Payment history matters most.
  • Small balances are easier to manage.
  • Slow applications beat random applications.

Plain-English guide

Keep the system simple enough to repeat.

01

Start with a student, secured, or relationship card.

02

Use it for predictable spending like gas, food, or subscriptions.

03

Keep old no-fee accounts open when possible.

Simple example

A $300 secured card used for one small subscription can build history if it is paid in full and kept current.

Common mistakes

  • Missing a due date.
  • Opening cards because of social media.
  • Letting a small balance become debt.
